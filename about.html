My Story

From apprenticeship to studio founder

Eight years of learning, building, and serving clients — here's the journey in three chapters.

2017 — The Beginning

I started in 2017 printing flyers and business cards out of Kaduna. I learned CorelDRAW, Photoshop, and Pixellab from scratch — designing for local businesses, schools, and event planners. This is where I learned the foundations of design and print production that still inform every project I touch today.

2018–2021 — Going Freelance

By 2018, I was freelancing full-time — picking up web development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) and combining it with my existing design skills. I served clients across Kaduna, Lagos, and Abuja with logos, flyers, business cards, social media graphics, and large-format print. Built a reputation for fast turnaround and bold designs.

2022 — Founding the Studio

In 2022 I founded Akay Mighty Concept — a one-stop creative studio for websites, brand identity, and print. Today the studio manages 150+ client projects across Nigeria and internationally, with a focus on conversion-driven websites, bold visual identities, and high-quality print production for businesses of all sizes.

Today & What's Next

Right now I'm focused on building websites that don't just look good — they convert. I'm also building a small productized-service line so more small businesses can access premium web + brand work without paying agency prices. If you're reading this and you have a project, let's talk.
